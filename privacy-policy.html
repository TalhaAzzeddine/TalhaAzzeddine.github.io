2. Information that may be handled

Most advertising-related information described below may be collected automatically by advertising SDKs integrated into a game. Depending on the relevant provider, game configuration, and device settings, this may include:

  • Device and technical information: device model and manufacturer, operating system and version, language, region, time zone, network connection, and related technical signals.
  • Identifiers and network information: IP address, Android advertising ID or other advertising/device identifiers, app-set identifiers, and signals used to recognize or protect an advertising session. An IP address may be used to estimate a device's general location.
  • App and advertising activity: app launches, ad impressions, taps, video views, rewarded-ad events, and other interactions needed to serve ads and measure their performance.
  • Diagnostics and security signals: information about SDK or app performance, errors, crashes, and signals used to detect invalid traffic, abuse, or fraud.
  • Information you send directly: if you email me, I receive your email address and the contents of your message so I can respond.

Viewing an in-game advertisement does not, by itself, require you to provide me with your name, phone number, or postal address. If a particular game offers an account, online, purchase, or other optional feature, that feature may involve additional data handling and should be reviewed alongside this policy and any notice shown in that game.

3. How information may be used

Information handled through the games and their advertising SDKs may be used to:

  • request, display, and limit the frequency of advertisements;
  • show personalized, contextual, or non-personalized ads, as permitted by your choices and applicable law;
  • measure ad delivery, views, clicks, and campaign performance;
  • maintain service quality, diagnose technical issues, and improve advertising systems;
  • detect invalid traffic, abuse, security issues, or fraud; and
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce applicable terms.

Where required by applicable law, the relevant consent or privacy-choice process must be provided and respected for advertising activities that require it.

5. Sharing and international transfers

Advertising SDKs may transmit relevant information to Google, AppLovin, and other parties involved in delivering, measuring, or protecting advertising services. Those parties may include advertising networks, demand partners, service providers, and fraud-prevention or measurement partners, according to their respective policies and agreements.

Information may also be disclosed where reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to a valid legal request, protect users, investigate fraud, or protect rights and security. Advertising providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live, subject to their policies and applicable safeguards.

I do not directly sell the information you send to me by email. However, advertising-related processing by third parties may be considered “sharing” or “sale” under some privacy laws, particularly when it supports interest-based advertising. See the provider policies and privacy controls linked above for details.

6. Your advertising and privacy choices

You may have options to limit personalized advertising or manage advertising identifiers. The available controls depend on your device, location, the game, and the provider.

  • Android settings: open your device Settings and search for “Ads” or “Advertising ID” to view the controls available on your Android version. You may be able to reset or delete the advertising ID or limit its use.
  • Google advertising choices: visit Google My Ad Center or review Google's advertising information.
  • AppLovin choices: review the AppLovin Privacy Policy for its available opt-out and data-access tools.
  • Consent controls: where a game presents a consent or privacy-options form, you can use that form to review or change the choices it supports.

Limiting personalized advertising does not necessarily remove ads; you may still see contextual or non-personalized advertisements. Device controls may also differ in what information they limit.

7. Data retention and deletion

I keep messages sent directly to me only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain relevant records, or comply with legal obligations.

Advertising providers determine how long they retain information collected through their SDKs, subject to their own policies and legal requirements. I do not control all of those retention periods. To request access to or deletion of advertising data held by a provider, use the provider's privacy policy and tools or contact me for guidance.

If a particular game supports account creation and you want to delete that account or associated data, email me from the address below with the game's name and any relevant player ID. Do not send passwords or other sensitive credentials. Requests will be handled in accordance with applicable law and the data the game actually allows me to control.

8. Children's privacy

The intended audience and age rating may differ from one game to another; please refer to the relevant Google Play listing. I do not knowingly ask children to send personal information directly to me through the games.

Where a game is directed to children or intended for a mixed audience, its advertising SDKs, consent flow, data practices, and ad content must be configured to meet applicable child-privacy laws and Google Play Families requirements. Parents or guardians who believe a child has sent personal information directly to me may contact me so I can review the request.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of certain personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, or to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To make a request about information I control, contact me using the email below and identify the relevant game. I may need to ask for reasonable information to locate the request and protect your privacy. For information collected independently by Google, AppLovin, or other advertising partners, you may need to submit a request directly to that provider. Some rights are subject to legal exceptions.

10. Security and policy changes

I take reasonable steps to protect information that I handle directly. Advertising providers describe their own technical and organizational security measures in their respective policies and documentation. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

This policy may be updated if the games, advertising SDKs, legal requirements, or data practices change. The “Last updated” date at the top indicates when the policy was most recently revised. Continued use of a game after an update is subject to the revised policy to the extent permitted by law.
